## Experiments

Three training seeds compare the same two feature sets: original 65 versus original 65 plus 12 additional features. Each trial uses eight threads and at most 1,200 trees per arm. Early stopping and threshold selection use separate entity sets. Full candidate truth is retained for the macro F0.5 calculation, including singletons and missing retrieved links.

Training uses 187,010 pairs from 20,000 references; early stopping and selection each use 100,000 candidate pairs from 2,500 references. These are existing development references and pre-bridge candidates. These pilot scores are not directly comparable to the final pipeline's 0.979820 development score or the 0.978547 fresh audit result. No submission file is generated by these experiments.

Six synthetic tests passed both locally and on the new VM. Training validates transferred data and truth hashes before fitting. Source hashes and trial protocols are recorded with the outputs. `run_cpu_trials.sh` refuses to overwrite completed or partial output directories.
